How it was tested

  • — plan days from — to —: your transcribed PharmD letters and the per-contract ES and NQ 5m bars in pharmd-bars, never spliced across a roll. Levels and symbolic refs are resolved on the contract the letter quotes.
  • Your PharmD fade trades (holds, LBAFs, LAAFs, failed reclaims): — setups, — touched after the letter was posted. Every plan level, faded from the side price came from: — setups, — touched.
  • Entries from the letter's posting time to — ET, flat at the — bar's close, one trade per setup per trigger.
  • A trigger is known only at the close of its own bar; the order goes in at the next 5m open plus — ticks. A resting limit fills only when price trades a tick through it. Stops sit one tick past the trigger's swing low or high, never closer than — × ATR5, and a bar that opens through the stop fills at its open.
  • Target: PharmD's first target at least —R away; a trade with no target at —R or more is skipped, as in your rule. Targets need a tick of trade-through. The stop is checked before the target inside a bar.
  • At the level means the swing low or high sits no more than — × ATR5 past the far edge and no more than — × ATR5 short of the near edge.
  • Divergence: a swing low that undercuts the previous swing low (the lowest bar of five on each side), — to — bars back, while the oscillator makes a higher low; mirrored for shorts. RSI(14), the MACD(12,26,9) line and its histogram, confirmed by one or two bars that hold the low.
  • Random-timing benchmark, one per trigger: on every touched setup, enter at a delay after the touch and with a stop size drawn from that trigger's own trades, same targets and costs. Its only difference from the trigger is that it ignores price, so the gap between the two is what the trigger's timing is worth.
